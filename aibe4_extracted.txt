Solution: Step 1: Understanding Defamation in Tort Law: In tort law, defamation refers to making false statements about someone that damage their reputation. Step 2: Detailed Explanation of the Options: - (A) Intention to defame is not necessary: This is the correct answer. Step 3: Final Conclusion: The correct answer is (A) Intention to defame is not necessary, as defamation focuses on the defamatory content of the statement, not on the intent behind it.
Quick Tip In defamation cases, the defendant can be held liable even if there was no intention to harm the plaintiff's reputation, as long as the statement was defamatory.
